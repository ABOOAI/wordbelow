# 词下图标

本项目图标使用内置 **imagegen** 生成；Chrome 16、48、128 像素图标与介绍页 256 像素图标均由同一原稿等比缩小得到。`icon-master.png` 是选用原稿。

设计方向：英文 A 在上、中文“文”在下，上半电光蓝底杏橙色字，下半杏橙色底蓝色字；上下共用两种颜色，形成正负形的联动，采用居中、简约无衬线字形。

最终生成提示词：

> Edit the split A / 文 icon by changing colors only. Preserve the exact glyph shapes, shared centerline, typography and 50/50 horizontal split. Upper half: solid energetic electric ultramarine #2724C9 background and warm apricot-orange #FFB35C A. Lower half: the same apricot-orange background and the same ultramarine 文. Maintain the two-color positive / negative relationship, centered glyphs and matching stroke weight. Full-bleed square, no white margins or transparent corners. Modern, energetic, refined and high contrast. Uniform flat colors, no gradients, noise, texture, extra shapes, labels, shadow, bevel or 3D. Preserve exact A and 文.
